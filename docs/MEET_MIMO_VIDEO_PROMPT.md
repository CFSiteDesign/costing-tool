# Claude Design prompt: "Meet MIMO" video

Upload with this prompt: `public/mm-head.png` (MIMO's monkey head), `public/mm-logo.png` (Mad Monkey logo, dark),
`public/mm-logo-white.png` (logo for dark backgrounds).

---

Make a 75 to 90 second "Meet MIMO" explainer video, 16:9, for the Mad Monkey Hostels team (general managers at
hostels across Southeast Asia, and head office). It introduces MIMO, the assistant built into their Experience
Database, and shows exactly how she works. The audience are busy hostel managers, not tech people. The tone is
Mad Monkey: warm, quick, a bit cheeky, never corporate, never "AI magic".

## Who MIMO is
MIMO is the assistant inside the Mad Monkey Experience Database, the tool where every hostel keeps its tours,
nightly events, third-party trips and transport, with prices, days and times. She is the sharpest person on the
hostel team: she knows every tour, every price and every day of the week, gets things done fast, and has a bit
of Mad Monkey cheek without ever making anyone feel daft. She is referred to as "she". Her face is the monkey
head in the uploaded file mm-head.png. Use that file as she is, do not redraw or restyle her, and place the
Mad Monkey logo file as a real image, never a generated lookalike.

## What she does (all true, do not add features)
1. You type what needs changing in plain words, the way you would text a colleague:
   "Pub crawl is 12 bucks now", "Move karaoke to 9pm", "Kulen runs mon wed fri", "Pause games night".
2. She works out which product you mean, even from nicknames and typos ("wens pub", "the quiz").
3. She shows a card with the change spelled out, old value to new value, and waits.
   Nothing changes until you press Confirm. Big changes (a 40 percent price jump, pausing something, renaming)
   ask "Sure?" first.
4. Confirm applies it through the normal system, so the change log, the re-verify flag and the note to head
   office all happen as they would if you had edited the form. Undo puts it back.
5. She answers questions from the same list: "What's on tonight?", "How much is the sunrise tour?",
   "Does each experience have a ticket limit?"
6. She remembers the nicknames your team uses. Confirm "wens pub" once and next time it's an exact match.
7. She lives in the sidebar, bottom left, or press Cmd K on any page. There is a fullscreen mode.
8. She greets you by name and by time of day: "Good morning, Kassy. What needs changing at Siem Reap?"

## What she never does (say this plainly in the video, it is the point)
- She never changes anything on her own. Every change is a proposal you confirm.
- She never deletes. "Delete" becomes "pause", and it stays in the database.
- She can only change price, times, days, status, website listing, name, description, meeting point, guest
  limits and expiry. Category changes and quotes are done on the product page or by head office.
- She only sees your own hostel's list.

## Real lines to use on screen (these are her actual replies, keep them verbatim)
- Greeting: "Good morning, Kassy. What needs changing at Siem Reap?"
- Proposal: "Pub Crawl goes from $10 to $12. Confirm below."
- Time shift: "Karaoke night moves to 9pm to midnight, keeping the same three hours. Confirm below."
- Two changes at once: "Kulen Waterfalls Tour goes from $40 to $45, and days change from every day to
  Mon, Wed, Fri. Confirm below."
- Question: "Tonight, Sunday: Canva painting roulette 5pm, Pool BBQ 6:30pm, Sunday funday package 7pm."
- Delete request: "Nothing gets deleted here, so I will pause Music Quiz instead."
- While she works, a line rotates under the bobbing monkey head: "Checking the going rate at Siem Reap",
  "Getting the monkeys in a row", "Flicking through the tour desk", "Counting the bananas".
- Applied: "Done: Pub Crawl $10.00 to $12.00. Reden gets a note. Undo"

## Suggested structure (adjust timing, keep the order)
1. Open (0 to 8s). Dark background, the Mad Monkey logo file top left, the monkey head fades in with the
   morning greeting typed out. Title: "Meet MIMO". Voice: "This is MIMO. She looks after the experiences at
   your hostel."
2. The problem (8 to 18s). A quick montage of the old way: a form with twelve fields, a spreadsheet, a message
   to head office asking for a price change. Voice: "Changing a price used to mean a form, a spreadsheet and a
   message to someone else. Now you just tell MIMO."
3. The one-line change (18 to 38s). Screen recording style: the sidebar button, the panel slides in, someone
   types "the pub crwal is 12 bucks now", the thinking line rotates, the card appears with $10 to $12, they
   press Confirm, the green done line with Undo. Voice: "Type it the way you'd say it. Typos and nicknames are
   fine. She shows you exactly what will change, and nothing moves until you confirm."
4. Questions (38 to 50s). "What's on tonight?" answered with the run sheet. Voice: "Ask her anything about your
   list. Tonight, tomorrow, prices, days, limits."
5. The guard rails (50 to 66s). Three quick cards: Never changes anything alone. Never deletes. Only your
   hostel. Voice: "She never acts alone, she never deletes, and she only sees your hostel. Big changes get a
   second check."
6. Learning and close (66 to 85s). "wens pub" confirmed once, next time an exact match. Voice: "She learns
   the names your team uses. Find her bottom left, or press Command K. Say good morning."
   End card: monkey head, "MIMO", the logo file, "Inside the Mad Monkey Experience Database".

## Look and feel
- Match the app: clean, lots of white space, Inter typeface, near-black text #1A1A18 on off-white #F4F3EF,
  Mad Monkey yellow #FFC72E as the single accent (used for the active nav bar and small highlights, never
  as a background flood). User messages are small grey chips on the right; MIMO's replies are plain text with
  the small monkey head on the left. Cards have a hairline border and three buttons: Confirm, Change, Cancel.
- Motion: tasteful and quick. The monkey head bobs gently while she thinks. Replies ease in. No shimmer, no
  particles, no robot or brain imagery, no glowing "AI" effects.
- Sound: light, upbeat, hostel common-room energy, not corporate. A single friendly voice, British or
  Australian, unhurried.
- Captions on by default, plain sentences.

## Copy rules
British spelling. Short sentences. No em dashes anywhere, use commas or full stops. No emojis. At most one
exclamation mark in the whole video. Never call her "AI-powered", "smart", "intelligent" or "magic". Never
say she "understands" or "thinks". Say what she does. Never show a change happening without a Confirm press.
