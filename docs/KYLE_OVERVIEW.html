Mad Monkey Experience Database

How the Experience Database works

A briefing for Kyle, 20 October 2026. What it is, how each team uses it, how it's being used so far, and where things stand. Live at mm-costing-tool.vercel.app.

What it is

One place for every tour, nightly event, one-off party and transfer at all 23 Mad Monkey hostels, with prices, days and times. It replaced the experience flow chart spreadsheet. GMs keep their own hostel up to date, head office builds group trip quotes from it, and the website is kept in line with it.

How a GM uses it

Their list

  • Every experience at their hostel, with search, filters and a day picker.
  • Prices, days and status change right in the table; everything else on the experience page. Nothing saves until they press Save.
  • Every edit clears the verified date until they check it and mark it verified.
  • New events through a short "Add product" form. One-offs drop off after their date.

The Assistant

  • They type a change in plain words ("move pub quiz to 830"). It shows the change, old value to new, and nothing happens until they press Confirm. Undo puts it back.
  • It answers questions about their list ("what's on tonight?") and brand questions from the marketing team's knowledge.
  • It only sees their hostel and never deletes anything.

My week and the weekly story

  • What's on each day, in time order.
  • Up to 3 key events a day, which build the hostel's weekly Instagram story (1080 x 1920), with quick text changes in plain words.

Global events, transport, activity

  • Events head office launches for every hostel arrive at the top of their list, with an email: Accept, Edit or Decline.
  • Transport lists every transfer with departure times. Activity logs every change. Export downloads the list as a spreadsheet.

Head office and sales

Head office

  • Global events: create once (date, price or "each hostel sets it", descriptions, assets, a message to GMs), send to every hostel, and see who accepted, declined or hasn't replied.
  • Approvals: changes picked up from the old flow chart and suggested start and finish times wait here until approved.
  • Activity: the full change log across every hostel.

Sales: the costing tool

  • Quotes built day by day from the live product list, or auto-created from stops and dates. Room rates sync every night from the rate sheet.
  • Line and whole-quote discounts, totals per person and per category. Agreement versions lock the prices behind a client link.
  • A client-ready day-by-day itinerary is written from the quote, then edited and shared.
  • New this week, from Hayley's feedback: quote notes, an Accepted status, a one-click booking request PDF for Reservations, and a payment reminder email 7 days before the deadline.

Behind the scenes

  • Emails go out from Mad Monkey's own verified domain, experience.madmonkeyhostels.com: logins, global events and payment reminders.
  • Room rates sync nightly from the rate sheet (67 successful syncs since 24 August). Exchange rates refresh daily, with manual overrides.
  • ALL IN trip bookings sync in every 5 minutes, and other Mad Monkey apps can read a guest's trip with their own revocable key.
  • Everyone only sees what their role allows. Keys and passwords are stored encrypted and can't be read back.

Where things stand

  • Shared admin login. Quotes are currently made on the shared admin account, so the change log and payment reminders can't tell who did what. Personal logins for everyone fix that.
  • Reden's website-change emails. Built and ready, currently switched off. 509 changes have queued up since launch and would be cleared before switching on a daily digest.
  • Approvals. 313 suggested start and finish times from the September flow chart import are waiting. About two thirds contradict the flow chart's own times and can be cleared, leaving the rest for review.
  • Private room types. Hayley wants Single, Double, Twin and Triple rooms. The rate sheet has one Private rate per hostel, so these need their own rates before quotes can use them.
  • GM rollout. A GM walkthrough video, slides and a two-page GM guide are ready. Luke, now an admin, can support onboarding.
