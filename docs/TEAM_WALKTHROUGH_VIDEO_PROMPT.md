---

Make a 5 to 6 minute walkthrough video, 16:9, with a voice-over and captions, for the whole Mad Monkey Hostels team:
hostel general managers (GMs), head office and the sales team. It walks through everything the Experience Database
does, at madmonkeyhostels.com/experiencedashboard. The viewers are busy, not technical, and many speak English as a
second language. Keep every sentence short and plain, one idea per scene, warm and a little cheeky, never corporate.
The video only explains how the software works: no questions, requests, decisions or calls to action.

Before designing any Mad Monkey branded frame, fetch https://design.madmonkeyhostels.com/llms.txt and follow it. Its
rules override this brief for brand frames (open, chapter cards, close).

## Everything it does (all true; do not add features, numbers or screens)

**Getting in (everyone)**
- Each person gets an email from updates@experience.madmonkeyhostels.com called "Your login for the Mad Monkey
  Experience Database". The button opens "Choose your password": type it twice and press "Save and go in".
- After that, sign in at madmonkeyhostels.com/experiencedashboard with your work email and password.
- The first time you sign in, a short guided tour runs. Replay it from the menu under your name.
- GMs see their own hostel. If they run more than one, a hostel list at the top left switches between them. Head office
  and sales see every hostel.
- Left menu: Experiences, My week, Transport, Activity, and the Assistant at the bottom (Cmd K opens it anywhere).
  Sales and head office also see Quotes; head office also sees Global events and Approvals.

**Your hostel's list (GMs)** (screenshots 01, 03, 15)
- Experiences shows every tour and event at the hostel: search, category and status filters, and day letters
  (M T W T F S S) to see what runs on a given day. Prices, days and status can be changed right in the table.
- Click a name to open the full page: descriptions, meeting point, inclusions, guest numbers, photo album. Nothing
  saves until you press "Save".
- Every edit clears the verified date. Once the details are checked, press "Mark verified" (the shield).
- More than one price? Under Rate, press "More than one price? Add price options" and give each price a name, for
  example "Without bus tickets" and "With bus tickets". The main rate is still the one quotes use.
- For anything sold on the website, a line under "Listed on website" shows either "Website up to date" or "Website:
  waiting for Reden to copy 1 change across". Reden is head office and keeps the website and Rezdy in line.
- "Social asset" opens Mad Monkey Studio with the experience already filled in.
- To stop something, switch off Active or set it to Paused. Nothing is ever deleted.
- "Export" downloads the list as a spreadsheet.

**Adding something new (GMs)** (screenshot 02)
"Add product", then: Title, Category (Nightly Events for anything the hostel runs itself), Rate ("0 = free", local
currency or USD), Brief description (95 characters, it goes on the website word for word), Days it runs, Start and
Finish, guest limits, meeting point, inclusions, photo album link, "Starts as" Active or Paused, "One-time event" with
a date for one-off parties (it drops off by itself afterwards), "Bookable on the Mad Monkey website" only if guests can
book it online. Then "Add product". If anything required is missing, it says so before saving.

**The Assistant (everyone)** (screenshot 08)
- Bottom of the left menu, or Cmd K. It greets you by name and time of day.
- Type a change like a text message ("move pub quiz to 830"). It shows a card with the change spelled out and waits.
  Nothing changes until you press "Confirm", and "Undo" puts it back. Big changes ask "Yes, I'm sure" first.
- It answers questions about your hostel ("What's on tonight?") and Mad Monkey brand questions (logos, captions,
  hashtags, promo codes) from the marketing team's guide.
- It only sees your hostel, never deletes ("delete" becomes "pause"), and learns the nicknames your team uses.

**Events from head office (GMs)**
When head office launches an event for every hostel, it waits at the top of Experiences and the GM gets an email.
"Accept" adds it as it is, "Edit" lets you set your own price or time first, "Decline" if you're not running it.

**My week and the weekly story (GMs)** (screenshots 04, 05)
- My week shows everything running each day in start-time order. Mark up to 3 "Key" events a day; they stay key
  every week.
- "Weekly story" turns the key events into an Instagram story (1080 x 1920). A "Quick changes" box takes plain words
  like "Remove Tuesday's quiz", then "Download story (PNG)".

**Transport and Activity (everyone)** (screenshots 06, 07)
Transport lists every bus, boat and transfer with departure times and prices. Activity shows every change, who made it
and when, with the before and after.

**Head office** (screenshots 13, 14, 16, 17)
- Add a hostel: press the "+" next to the hostel list, type the name, pick the country (currency and time zone fill
  in by themselves), press "Add hostel". The new hostel opens straight away, ready for its experiences.
- Global events: "New global event" sends an event to every hostel at once, emails the GMs, and shows who accepted,
  declined or hasn't replied.
- Approvals > Website updates: every change to something sold on the website lands here and in Reden's inbox within
  15 minutes, with who changed it and when. Once the website and Rezdy match, press "Done on website". It moves to
  "Completed", which keeps who ticked it and when. "Move back" undoes a mis-tick.

**Sales: from quote to booking** (screenshots 09, 11, 10)
- Quotes: "New quote" with the customer's details, or "Auto-create trip" to build a whole day-by-day trip from the
  stops and nights.
- Each day has its stays, breakfast, experiences and transport, priced per person. Discounts are logged against your
  name. Notes can go on the whole booking, on each day and on each item.
- "Generate agreement version" freezes the prices into a numbered version with a link the client can open.
  "Detailed itinerary" writes a client-friendly day-by-day the client can read.
- "Mark accepted" on the version the client agreed to, then "Booking request" makes the PDF for Reservations, with
  guest details, payment deadline and pricing. The Mad Monkey contact gets an email 7 days before the deadline unless
  it's marked paid.

## Look and feel
- App screens must look like the uploaded screenshots: white background, near-black text #1A1A18, light grey sidebar,
  Inter typeface, hairline borders, small rounded status chips. Mad Monkey yellow #FFC72E is the only accent in the
  app. Animate them as a clean screen recording: a visible cursor, a soft zoom on the part that matters, a highlight
  ring on the button being pressed. Never invent buttons or screens that are not in the screenshots or this brief; for
  the few moments without a screenshot (the Choose your password page, the Assistant's card), build a simple mockup in
  the same style using only the words in this brief.
- Brand frames and chapter cards follow llms.txt: Mad Black #0a0a0a, Montserrat, lime #ccff01 and pink #ff01aa used
  sparingly, the "ALL IN" sticker at most once. Place the logo files as real images; never redraw, recolour or restyle
  the logo or the monkey head.
- Motion quick and tidy. No particles, glows, robots, brains or "AI" imagery.
- One friendly voice, unhurried, easy to follow for non-native English speakers. Light, upbeat hostel common-room
  music, low under the voice. Captions on throughout, matching the voice-over word for word.

## Copy rules
British spelling. Short sentences. No em dashes anywhere (use commas or full stops). No emojis. At most one
exclamation mark in the whole video. Never call the Assistant "AI-powered", "smart" or "magic", and never say it
"thinks" or "understands"; say what it does. Never show a change happening without a "Save", "Confirm" or "Done on
website" press. Use the exact button names from this brief.
