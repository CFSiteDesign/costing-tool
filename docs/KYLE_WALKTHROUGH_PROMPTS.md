PROMPT 1: VIDEO
======================================================================

Make a 2 minute overview video, 16:9, for Kyle, a senior leader at Mad Monkey Hostels, showing what the Experience
Database (mm-costing-tool.vercel.app) is and what it now does for the business. Tone: confident and plain, a product
tour for a decision maker. Short, factual, no hype, no "AI magic".

Before designing, fetch https://design.madmonkeyhostels.com/llms.txt and follow it for every branded frame. Its rules
override this brief.

The facts (all true, do not add features or numbers):
- It replaced the experience flow chart spreadsheet. Every tour, nightly event, one-off party and transfer for all 23
  hostels lives in one place: 1,355 products, 772 experiences running and 497 transport options.
- GMs keep their own hostel up to date: edit in the list, add new events, or tell the built-in Assistant in plain
  words. Nothing changes until they press Save or Confirm. Nothing is ever deleted, only paused. Every change is
  logged with who made it.
- In the last 30 days, 12 GMs made 270 changes. 35 GMs have logins.
- My week shows what's on each day, and turns each hostel's key events into a ready-made Instagram story.
- Head office launches global events (Halloween, Oktoberfest) once, and every hostel accepts, tweaks or declines.
- The costing tool sits on top: sales build group trip quotes day by day from the live product list, with room rates
  synced every night from the rate sheet. Agreement versions lock the prices for the client.
- New this week: when a client accepts, one click makes the booking request for Reservations (screenshot 10), and the
  Mad Monkey contact gets an email 7 days before payment is due.
- The system sends its own emails from experience.madmonkeyhostels.com: logins, global events and payment reminders.
