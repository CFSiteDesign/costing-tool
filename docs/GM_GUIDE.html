Mad Monkey Experience Database

GM quick guide

Every tour, event and transfer your hostel runs, in one place. Sign in at mm-costing-tool.vercel.app with your work email and your password.

Add a new event Most used

  1. Open Experiences in the left menu and press Add product at the top right.

  2. Title: the name guests see, like Beer Pong or Sunset Hour.

  3. Category: Nightly Events for anything the hostel runs itself. Use MM Tours, 3rd Party Tours or Transport for the rest.

  4. Rate: the price per person. Type 0 if it's free. You can enter it in your local currency or in USD.

  5. Brief description: one short line, 95 characters at most. It goes on the website word for word.

  6. Days it runs: tap each day it happens, then set the Start and Finish time.

  7. Extras if you have them: long description, meeting point and time, inclusions, things to bring, guest numbers, and a photo album link with at least 6 photos.

  8. Starts as: Active if it's running now, Paused if it's ready but not running yet.

  9. One-off event (Halloween, Tequila Day)? Turn on One-time event and pick the date. It disappears on its own after that day.

  10. Only turn on Bookable on the Mad Monkey website if guests can book it online.

  11. Press Add product. If something important is missing, it tells you what before saving.

Change an event

  • Open it from Experiences, change what you need, then press Save.
  • Then press Mark verified to show it's been checked. Every save clears the old check.
  • Stopping an event: set its Status to Paused, or switch off Active in the list. Nothing is ever deleted.

Or just ask the Assistant

  • Click Assistant at the bottom of the left menu and type in plain words: "Move pub quiz to Tuesday at 8pm".
  • It shows you the change first. Nothing changes until you press Confirm.
  • Ask it questions too: "What's on tonight?" or "What runs on Saturday?"

Events from head office

  • When head office launches an event for every hostel, it shows at the top of Experiences.
  • Accept adds it as it is. Edit lets you change the price or time for your hostel first. Decline if you're not running it.

My week and the weekly story

  • My week shows what's on each day, in time order.
  • Tick Key on up to 3 events a day. They stay key every week until you untick them.
  • Press Weekly story for an Instagram story of your key events. Type changes like "Remove Tuesday's quiz", then Download story (PNG).

Also handy

  • More than one hostel? Switch between them with the hostel dropdown at the top left.
  • Transport: your transfers and buses, with routes, times and prices.
  • Activity: who changed what, and when.
  • Export on the Experiences page downloads your hostel's list as a spreadsheet.
